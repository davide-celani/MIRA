# Code of Conduct

MIRA welcomes participation from people of all backgrounds and levels of
experience. This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests,
discussions, and other project-related interactions.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and use constructive, clear language.
- Discuss ideas, methods, and code without personal attacks.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes, and respect differing perspectives.
- Respect confidentiality and the privacy of contributors and research
  participants.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexualized content or
unwanted attention, and publication of another person's private information
without permission are unacceptable. Repeated disruption and abusive behavior
are also unacceptable.

## Reporting and response

Report concerns privately to the maintainer, Davide Celani, at
<davide.celani.00@gmail.com>. Include the relevant context and links where
possible. Reports will be handled with discretion; information may be shared
only as needed to investigate and respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed.

The maintainer may ask for clarification, request a change in behavior, remove
inappropriate content, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently.
The response should consider the nature, impact, and repetition of the
behavior. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is unacceptable.

This is a project-specific policy. It does not imply affiliation with an
external enforcement body.
